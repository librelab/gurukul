Rishaan has participated in various Gurukul(Home-education) based activities which covers the outcomes in the KLA as outlined by NESA. Reports based on KLA is underlined below.

HT1-1<br>
-----------
1. **communicates an understanding of change and continuity in family life using appropriate historical terms**
 
 
#### Activity:
 The student explored history by examining people around and comparing past and present lifestyles. He used historical vocabulary such as past, present, change, before, after, generation, and tradition during discussions.
 
#### Example:
 Rishaan observes and asks questions to us, about how back in India we grew up, studied, our culture. He is able to comprehend that eventhough he is in different land with its unique set of inhabitants, he has his roots back in another land with their own set of culture and history.
 
#### Observation:

He confidently uses appropriate historical terms to describe events, sequences, and comparisons between the past and present. He communicates his ideas clearly through discussions, drawings.

2. **identifies and describes significant people, events, places and sites in the local community over time**
 
#### Activity:

#### Example:

3. **describes the effects of changing technology on people's lives over time 

Activity:
 The student explored how technology has changed over time by comparing old and modern household items, communication devices, transport, and everyday tools. He used books, photographs, videos, and discussions to investigate how technology has improved daily life.
 
Example:	
 Rishaan compared walkie talkie and modern smartphone. He identified differences in their features eventhough both of them uses radio technology. He likes to watch the  video of "evolution of planes" in which he could enumerate jets name and compare the mach speed of some of the figter jets.
 
Observation:
 The student demonstrates an understanding that technology changes over time and influences the way people live, work, travel, and communicate. He can identify examples of older and newer technologies and describe how these changes have made everyday tasks easier and more efficient.
